Questions, Answered

Clear Answers Before You Begin

Every case is different. These general answers help you understand what to expect before an expert reviews your requirements.

Which documents are required to start?
The documents required depend on the specific service. For GST registration, you typically need PAN, Aadhaar, business address proof, and bank account details. Our team will provide a complete checklist after understanding your specific requirements.
Can the complete process be handled online?
Yes, our entire process is 100% online. You can submit documents securely through our portal, track application status in real-time, and receive all certificates and deliverables digitally. No office visit required.
How long does GST registration normally take?
With complete and correct documents, GST registration is typically approved within 3–5 working days. We handle all communication with the GST department and resolve any queries promptly to ensure timely approval.
Which ITR form is applicable to me?
The correct ITR form depends on your income sources. ITR-1 is for salaried individuals, ITR-2 for capital gains, ITR-3 for business income, and so on. Our CA will assess your income sources and select the most appropriate form for accurate filing.
Can you help if I have received a GST notice?
Yes, we handle all types of GST notices including DRC-01, ASMT-10, and SCN notices. Our team reviews the notice, reconciles your records, prepares a legally appropriate response, and represents you in department proceedings if required.
Do you provide PMEGP and Mudra loan documentation support?
Yes, we provide end-to-end support for PMEGP and Mudra loan applications including DPR preparation, CMA data, projected financials, bank liaison, and portal application. We help maximize your subsidy eligibility.
What is the difference between Basic, State and Central FSSAI licenses?
Basic FSSAI registration is for businesses with annual turnover below ₹12 lakhs. State license is required for turnover between ₹12 lakhs and ₹20 crores. Central license is needed for turnover above ₹20 crores or businesses operating across states.
Is IEC registration valid for a lifetime?
Yes, an IEC (Import Export Code) is valid for the lifetime of the business entity and does not require renewal. However, annual update/verification through the DGFT portal is mandatory. We help with both new IEC registration and annual updates.
How will I receive updates about my application?
You will receive real-time updates via WhatsApp, email, and our client portal. Your dedicated expert will keep you informed at every stage — from document submission to final approval.
Are professional and government fees charged separately?
Yes, our professional service fees and government statutory fees are quoted separately for full transparency. You will receive a complete fee breakdown before any work begins, so there are no surprises.
