Refund Policy

How to raise a cancellation request, payment concern or refund query.

Last updated: 12 October 2026
On this page
01   Scope of this policy02   Cancellation before work begins03   Work already performed04   Government and third-party payments05   Duplicate payments and service concerns06   How to request a review07   Confirmation and payment processing

01Scope of this policy

This policy covers requests relating to services purchased from Bakhetia & Co. The quotation, invoice and written engagement terms are important when reviewing a request. This policy does not limit any rights available under applicable law.

02Cancellation before work begins

Contact us as soon as possible if you want to cancel. We will review whether work has started and whether any authorised expenses have already been incurred, and explain the proposed refund or adjustment in writing.

03Work already performed

If work has begun, the review will take into account the agreed scope, work actually completed and documented third-party expenses. There is no automatic blanket forfeiture stated by this policy. Ask for a breakdown if you need clarification about a proposed deduction.

04Government and third-party payments

Fees already paid to a government department, bank or another provider are subject to that recipient's rules and recovery process. Any amount recovered or not yet paid should be considered in the refund review. Please request a breakdown of professional fees and external charges.

05Duplicate payments and service concerns

If you believe you have paid twice, paid an incorrect amount or received a service that does not match the agreement, share the payment reference and details of the issue. We will review the records and discuss a correction, completion of the agreed work or an appropriate refund where due.

06How to request a review

Email fcpco7303@gmail.com with your name, registered phone number, service requested, invoice or transaction reference, payment date and reason for the request. Do not email full card details, bank passwords or OTPs. You can also call +91 8396990790 for assistance.

07Confirmation and payment processing

The outcome, any applicable deductions and expected processing time will be communicated after review. Where a refund is approved, the payment method will be confirmed with you; bank or payment-provider processing times may vary. No fixed refund period is promised on this page.
